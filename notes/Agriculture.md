# Agriculture

**Summary**: Notes on agricultural mapping, crop monitoring and field-level analysis.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [agribound: Agricultural field boundary delineation from satellite imagery](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): A [[Python]] package that maps field boundaries from [[Remote_Sensing]] imagery (Landsat, Sentinel-2, NAIP and others) or [[Embeddings]]. It runs models such as Delineate-Anything, Fields of The World, Prithvi and DINOv3 through one pipeline and exports polygons as GeoParquet or GeoPackage. Related: [[Deep_Learning]].
  **Keywords**: field boundaries, parcel delineation, Python package, foundation models, Google Earth Engine, SAM
